# MedMemory

Version: 1.0
Date: June 2026

## Vision

MedMemory is a secure digital health vault that helps Indian families store, organize, search, and understand medical records across a lifetime.

## Why We Exist

Healthcare records are fragmented across hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers, and paper files.

Patients and caregivers often:

- lose important documents
- repeat tests unnecessarily
- struggle to explain medical history
- spend hours searching for records

This becomes especially painful during chronic illnesses such as:

- Cancer
- Diabetes
- Hypertension
- Heart Disease
- Kidney Disease

MedMemory solves this problem by creating a lifelong digital health memory.

## Product Promise

Never lose your medical history again.

## Target User

Primary:

Adult child managing healthcare records for parents.

Secondary:

- Individual patients
- Caregivers
- Families with chronic illnesses

## MVP Scope

Users can:

- Create an account
- Create family member profiles
- Upload medical documents
- Extract information using AI
- Review extracted information
- Search medical history
- View a chronological health timeline

## Out of Scope

- Diagnosis
- Treatment recommendations
- Telemedicine
- Doctor booking
- Wearable integration
- Insurance claims
- Mobile applications

## Long-Term Vision

Become the personal health operating system for families.

Users should eventually be able to:

- Store all records
- Track health trends
- Share information with doctors
- Manage appointments
- Manage insurance
- Receive personalized health insights
